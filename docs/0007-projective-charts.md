# Standard affine charts of projective space

PR #8 constructs the finite standard affine open cover of projective space
over an arbitrary field, using pinned mathlib v4.33.0.

## Coordinate opens and the covering argument

For `Pⁿ_k = Proj k[X₀, …, Xₙ]`, define the standard open `D₊(Xᵢ)`.
The coordinate index is `ULift (Fin (n + 1))`, matching the existing
homogeneous coordinate ring and retaining support for every field universe.

The key algebraic result is
`ProjectiveSpace.irrelevant_eq_span_coordinates`: the irrelevant ideal is
the ideal generated by the homogeneous coordinates. For the forward
inclusion, every positive-degree homogeneous polynomial has zero
coefficients in degree zero and therefore belongs to the variable ideal.
For the reverse inclusion, each coordinate is homogeneous of degree one.

Mathlib's covering theorem then gives
`ProjectiveSpace.iSup_coordinateOpen_eq_top`: the union of all the standard
coordinate opens is the whole projective scheme.

## Affine chart rings

`ProjectiveSpace.ChartRing k n i` is the homogeneous localization
`(k[X₀, …, Xₙ]_{Xᵢ})₀`. Its elements are degree-zero fractions represented
by homogeneous numerators and powers of `Xᵢ` of the same degree.

Because `Xᵢ` is homogeneous of positive degree, mathlib supplies the
canonical isomorphism

```lean
ProjectiveSpace.coordinateOpenIsoSpec k n i :
  (ProjectiveSpace.coordinateOpen k n i).toScheme ≅
    ProjectiveSpace.chartScheme k n i
```

Here `chartScheme` is the spectrum of `ChartRing`.
The file also exposes `isAffineOpen_coordinateOpen`, the open immersion
`chartι`, and the fact that its image is exactly `coordinateOpen`.

## Compatibility with the field structure

The chart ring's `k`-algebra structure is the composite of the coefficient
map into the degree-zero ring and its map into the homogeneous localization.
An `IsScalarTower` instance records that compatibility.

The structural morphism `chartStructureMap` is `Spec.map` of this coefficient
map. The theorem `chartι_structureMap` proves

```lean
ProjectiveSpace.chartι k n i ≫ Variety.projectiveStructureMap k n =
  ProjectiveSpace.chartStructureMap k n i
```

Thus the affine charts carry the same base-field structure as projective
space. This equation will be needed when transferring geometric properties
from the charts to projective space.

## The packaged cover

`ProjectiveSpace.coordinateAffineOpenCover k n` is a mathlib
`Scheme.AffineOpenCover`. Its rings and morphisms reduce to the exported
chart rings and chart immersions. A `Fintype` instance and
`card_coordinateAffineOpenCover` record exactly `n + 1` indexed charts.

The construction includes `n = 0`; its cover has one chart.

## Next boundary

This slice constructs affine charts as spectra of homogeneous localizations.
The identification of each chart ring with a polynomial ring in `n`
variables remains to be proved over `k`. That identification will connect
these charts to our affine-space smoothness result and allow smoothness of
projective space to be proved locally. This slice does not yet claim either
that chart isomorphism or smoothness of projective space.
